The order screen is where your servers and cashiers build every ticket — selecting items, capturing guest preferences, and firing orders to the kitchen or bar with a single tap. Gratly keeps the process fast so your staff spends more time on the floor and less time staring at a screen.

## Opening an Order

You can start an order in two ways:

- **Dine-in:** Tap a seated (yellow) table on the floor plan. If the table has no order yet, a blank order screen opens automatically.
- **Walk-up / Takeout:** Tap **+ New Order** on the home screen to open a fresh order not tied to a table.

Both paths lead to the same order-building screen.

## Adding Items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Menu">
    Tap a category tab at the top of the order screen (e.g., **Starters**, **Mains**, **Drinks**) to filter the item grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Item">
    Tap the item you want to add. It is added to the order immediately if it has no required modifiers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Modifiers">
    If the item has required modifiers (e.g., doneness, size, or toppings), the modifier screen appears automatically. Select the appropriate options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Addition">
    Tap **Add to Order**. The item appears in the ticket panel on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding a Special Note

To capture a guest's specific request or flag an allergy, attach a free-text note directly to any line item:

1. Tap the line item in the ticket panel.
2. Tap **Add Note**.
3. Type the instruction (e.g., "no onions", "allergy: nuts", "dressing on the side").
4. Tap **Done**.

The note prints on the kitchen ticket and displays on the KDS alongside the item, so kitchen staff see it without any guesswork.

## Changing Quantities

- Tap **+** or **−** next to a line item to increase or decrease its quantity.
- To remove an item entirely, swipe left on the line item and tap **Remove**.

## Sending the Order to the Kitchen

When the order is ready to fire, tap **Send Order**. Gratly immediately pushes the ticket to the KDS screen and/or kitchen printer, depending on your hardware setup.

If you need to make a change after sending, tap **Edit Order**. Items the kitchen has not yet marked **In Progress** can still be modified or removed. Once an item is In Progress on the KDS, changes require a manager override.

## Takeout Orders

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a Takeout Order">
    Tap **+ New Order**, then select **Takeout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Customer Details">
    Optionally enter a customer name or phone number to identify the order at pickup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and Send the Order">
    Add items and tap **Send Order** as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Takeout orders appear in a dedicated **Takeout** queue on the KDS, keeping them visually separate from dine-in tickets so kitchen staff can prioritize and bag them correctly.

<Note>
  Orders cannot be deleted after being sent to the kitchen. To remove a sent item, tap the line item and select **Void Item** or **Comp Item** — both actions require a manager PIN.
</Note>

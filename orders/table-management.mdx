The floor plan view gives you a live, color-coded map of your entire dining room. At a glance, you can see which tables are open, which are mid-service, and which need immediate attention — so your front-of-house team stays coordinated without shouting across the room.

## The Floor Plan View

Every table on the floor plan displays a status color that updates in real time as your team works:

- **Green** — Available (no active order)
- **Yellow** — Occupied (order in progress)
- **Red** — Needs attention (order overdue or payment requested)
- **Gray** — Closed / reserved

Tap any table to view its current order, assign a server, or take action.

## Seating a Guest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Floor Plan">
    From the POS home screen, tap the **Floor Plan** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Table">
    Tap an available (green) table to open its detail panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seat the Party">
    Tap **Seat Guests**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Party Size">
    Enter the number of guests in the party and tap **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Table Is Now Active">
    The table turns yellow and is ready to receive an order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning a Server

After seating a party, tap **Assign Server** in the table detail panel and select a staff member from the list. Server attribution links every item and tip on that table to the assigned server, which flows directly into staff performance reports. You can reassign a server at any point during service without affecting the open order.

## Setting Up Your Floor Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Floor Plan Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Floor Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Table">
    Tap **+ Add Table**, then enter a table number or name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position the Table">
    Drag the table to its physical location on the layout grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Layout">
    Tap **Save Layout** to publish the changes to all terminals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can create multiple sections — for example, **Indoor**, **Outdoor**, and **Bar** — to mirror your physical space. Use the section tabs at the top of the floor plan screen to switch between areas during service. Each section has its own layout grid and can be configured independently.

## Transferring a Table

If a party moves to a different table, carry the open order with them without losing any items or modifications:

1. Tap the occupied (yellow) table on the floor plan.
2. Tap **Transfer**.
3. Select the destination table from the list of available tables.
4. Tap **Confirm Transfer**.

The order moves to the new table instantly and the original table returns to green.

<Tip>
  Floor plan changes — including table transfers and status updates — sync to all POS terminals instantly. Every team member sees the same live view.
</Tip>

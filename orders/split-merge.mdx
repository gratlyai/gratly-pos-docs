Large parties rarely pay as one. Gratly gives you three ways to split a check — by item, evenly, or by seat — so every guest pays exactly what they owe. When separate orders need to consolidate into a single bill, the merge function brings them together just as easily.

## Split by Item

Use this option when guests want to choose exactly which items appear on their individual checks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Tap the occupied table on the floor plan to open the active order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate a Split">
    Tap **Split Check**, then tap **By Item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute the Items">
    Drag each line item into the appropriate check column. Add more check columns as needed by tapping **+ Add Check**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Split">
    Tap **Confirm Split**. Each check is now independent and can be paid separately by the relevant guest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Split Evenly

Use this option when a party agrees to divide the total equally regardless of who ordered what.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Tap the occupied table on the floor plan to open the active order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate a Split">
    Tap **Split Check**, then tap **Evenly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Number of Ways">
    Enter the number of guests splitting the bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Split">
    Tap **Confirm**. Gratly divides the total equally and creates a separate check for each portion, ready for individual payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Split by Seat

Seat-based splitting works best when you assign items to seat numbers as you take the order. When building the order, tap a line item, then tap **Assign Seat** and enter the guest's seat number. Once every item is assigned, Gratly can group them automatically:

1. Open the order and tap **Split Check**.
2. Tap **By Seat**.
3. Gratly groups each item under its assigned seat number and creates a check per seat.
4. Tap **Confirm Split** to finalize.

This approach is especially useful for large parties where guests order at different times throughout the meal.

## Merging Checks

If two open orders at the same table need to become one bill — for example, when a couple decides to pay together after ordering separately — merge them in a few taps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open One of the Orders">
    Tap the table and open either of the two orders you want to combine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a Merge">
    Tap **Merge Check**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Second Order">
    From the list of open orders at the same table, tap the order you want to merge into the current one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Merge">
    Tap **Confirm Merge**. All items from both checks combine into a single order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Merging checks is not reversible. If you merge in error, you will need to re-split the combined check manually before processing payment.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Partial payments are fully supported — a guest can pay their portion of a split check while the remaining checks stay open for other guests to pay at their own pace.
</Tip>

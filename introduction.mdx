## How Gratly Works

Gratly is organized around the natural flow of restaurant service — from setup through each shift to closing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Account">
    Create your restaurant profile, add your menu, and configure your floor plan in **Settings**. Takes about 30 minutes for a typical menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Team">
    Create staff accounts and assign roles (cashier, server, manager). Each team member gets their own PIN login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Taking Orders">
    Tap a table on the floor plan, add items from the menu, and send the order to the kitchen — tickets appear on the Kitchen Display System instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close & Report">
    Accept payments, apply discounts, and print or email receipts. At the end of each shift, run your sales and end-of-day reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

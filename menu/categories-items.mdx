Categories and items are the foundation of your Gratly menu. Categories group your dishes into logical sections — like Starters, Mains, or Drinks — while items represent every individual dish or beverage your guests can order. Keeping your menu well-organized here makes order-taking faster for your staff and clearer for your kitchen.

## Creating a Category

Before you can add items, set up at least one category to hold them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Menu Categories">
    From the Gratly dashboard, go to **Menu > Categories**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new category">
    Tap **+ New Category** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the category">
    Enter a clear, guest-facing name for the category, such as **Mains**, **Starters**, or **Desserts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a color label">
    Select a color label to visually distinguish this category on the POS order screen. Each category can have a unique color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. The category appears immediately in your menu list and on all POS terminals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reordering Categories

The order categories appear in on the POS screen is fully customizable. In **Menu > Categories**, press and hold any category row, then drag it up or down to your preferred position. Release to drop it in place. Gratly saves the new order automatically — no save button required.

## Adding a Menu Item

With a category in place, you can start populating it with items.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a category">
    From **Menu > Categories**, tap the category you want to add an item to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new item">
    Tap **+ New Item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in item details">
    Enter the following details:
    - **Name** — The dish or drink name as it will appear on the POS and receipts (e.g., *House Burger*).
    - **Description** *(optional)* — A short description for staff reference (e.g., *8oz beef patty, lettuce, tomato, house sauce*).
    - **Base Price** — The standard price for this item before any modifiers or overrides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an image (optional)">
    Tap the image placeholder and select a photo from your device. Item images appear on the POS terminal to help staff identify dishes quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. The item is now available for ordering on all terminals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing an Item

To update any item's name, description, price, or image, tap the item's name in the category list. This opens the item in edit mode. Make your changes and tap **Save**. Updates take effect across all terminals immediately.

## Marking Items as Unavailable

When a dish runs out mid-service, use the availability toggle to pull it from the POS without deleting it.

On the item's detail screen, toggle the **Available** switch to the off position. The item turns gray in the menu and cannot be added to any new orders until you re-enable it. This is also known as "86'ing" an item.

To make the item available again, return to the item detail screen and toggle **Available** back on.

## Deleting Items and Categories

<Warning>
  Deleting a category permanently removes the category **and every item inside it**. This action cannot be undone. If you only need to pull items from ordering temporarily — such as a seasonal dish — mark them as unavailable instead of deleting them. Only delete a category when you're certain none of its items need to be recovered.
</Warning>

To delete a single item, open the item detail screen and tap **Delete Item** at the bottom. To delete a category, open **Menu > Categories**, swipe left on the category row, and tap **Delete**.

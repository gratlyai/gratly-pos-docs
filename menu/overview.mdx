Menu management in Gratly POS gives you full control over everything your guests can order. From the Menu section of your dashboard, you can build and update your entire menu — organizing dishes into categories, attaching customization options, and setting pricing rules — without any downtime or syncing delays.

## Menu Hierarchy

Gratly structures your menu in four layers, each building on the one above:

- **Categories** — Top-level groupings that organize your menu into sections (e.g., Starters, Mains, Drinks, Desserts)
  - **Items** — Individual dishes or beverages that belong to a category (e.g., Caesar Salad, House Burger, Sparkling Water)
    - **Modifiers** — Customization options attached to an item (e.g., Cook Level, Add-ons, Drink Size)
      - **Pricing** — A base price per item, with optional time-based or service-type price overrides (e.g., Happy Hour, Takeout surcharge)

Think of it as a tree: categories branch into items, items branch into modifiers, and pricing rules can apply at any level.

After every payment, Gratly gives you the flexibility to print a physical receipt, send a digital copy to the guest's email, or close the order silently — whatever fits your service style. You can set a default behavior for your whole location and customize exactly what appears on every receipt so it reflects your brand.

## Receipt Options

Once a payment is confirmed, Gratly presents three receipt delivery options:

- **Print receipt** — immediately sends the receipt to your connected receipt printer. The guest receives a paper copy at the table or counter.
- **Email receipt** — opens a keyboard prompt so the guest can enter their email address. Gratly sends a formatted digital receipt to that address within seconds.
- **No receipt** — closes the order without generating any receipt. Use this when guests decline a receipt to keep the line moving.

## Setting the Default Receipt Behavior

You can configure which option Gratly defaults to at checkout so your team doesn't have to make the choice on every transaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Receipt Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Receipts** from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a default behavior">
    Select one of the four default modes:
    - **Ask** — Gratly prompts the staff member to choose after each payment (recommended for most restaurants).
    - **Always Print** — automatically prints a receipt for every transaction.
    - **Always Email** — automatically prompts for an email address after every payment.
    - **None** — closes orders without a receipt unless the staff member manually requests one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your setting">
    Tap **Save**. The new default takes effect immediately on all terminals at your location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customizing Your Receipt

Make your receipts feel on-brand and give guests the information they need to contact you or return. All receipt customization lives in **Settings > Receipts > Customize**.

- **Restaurant name and address** — pulled automatically from your Gratly business profile. Update your profile to change these fields.
- **Logo** — upload a PNG or JPG file (maximum 500 KB) to display at the top of both printed and emailed receipts. For best results, use a square or landscape image with a white or transparent background.
- **Header message** — a short line of text printed above the itemized bill (for example, *"Thank you for dining with us!"* or *"Welcome to [Restaurant Name]"*).
- **Footer message** — a line of text printed below the total and payment summary. Use this for your website URL, social media handles, loyalty program instructions, or a return policy note.
- **Tax display** — choose whether to show or hide the tax line on the receipt, and customize the tax label (for example, "Sales Tax," "GST," or "VAT") to match your jurisdiction.

## Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT)

Kitchen Order Tickets are separate from guest receipts. A KOT prints automatically to your kitchen ticket printer — or displays on your Kitchen Display System (KDS) — the moment a server sends an order from the POS. KOTs give the kitchen everything they need to prepare the order without any guest-facing pricing or payment information.

Customize the KOT layout in **Settings > Printers > KOT Settings**:

- **Show/hide table number** — display the table number at the top of each ticket so kitchen staff know where dishes are going.
- **Show/hide server name** — include the server's name on the ticket to make it easy to coordinate questions between the floor and the kitchen.
- **Show/hide seat assignments** — when seat-level ordering is enabled, print which seat each item belongs to so runners can deliver dishes directly without asking.

<Tip>
  Collecting email addresses for digital receipts is a simple way to build your customer contact list over time. You can export all captured addresses from **Reports > Customer Data** to use in email marketing campaigns or loyalty program outreach.
</Tip>

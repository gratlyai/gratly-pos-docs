When a guest is ready to pay, Gratly POS guides you through collecting payment quickly and accurately — whether they're paying cash, tapping a card, or splitting the bill across multiple methods. Every transaction is recorded automatically so your end-of-day reports stay complete without extra work.

## Opening the Payment Screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    From the table layout or order list, tap the order you want to close out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Charge">
    Tap the **Charge** button at the bottom of the order screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the total due">
    The payment screen opens and displays the full balance owed, including taxes and any applied discounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Methods

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cash">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Cash">
        On the payment screen, tap **Cash**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the amount tendered">
        Type in the amount the guest is handing you, or tap one of the suggested denomination shortcuts to fill it in automatically.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm change due">
        Gratly calculates and displays the change amount on screen. Collect the cash and return the correct change to the guest.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete the payment">
        Tap **Confirm Payment** to close the order and mark it as paid.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Card (Tap / Chip / Swipe)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Card">
        On the payment screen, tap **Card**. The connected card reader activates and prompts the customer to present their card.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Customer presents card">
        The guest taps (contactless), inserts (chip), or swipes (magnetic stripe) their card on the reader.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for authorization">
        Gratly contacts the payment processor. Authorization typically completes within a few seconds. Do not remove the card until the reader signals approval.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish the transaction">
        Once approved, tap **Done**. The order closes and a receipt prompt appears.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Split Payment">
    When a guest wants to pay part of the bill in one method and the remainder in another, use the Split Payment flow.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Tap Split Payment">
        On the payment screen, tap **Split Payment** instead of selecting a single method.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the first payment amount">
        Enter the amount the guest wants to apply to the first method (for example, $20.00 on a gift card). Tap the payment method and process it as normal.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Process the first method">
        Complete the transaction for that method — collect cash, authorize the card, or redeem the gift card.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Process the remaining balance">
        Gratly automatically shows the remaining balance. Select the second payment method and process it to zero out the check.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    You can split across as many methods as needed. Gratly tracks each portion separately in transaction reports.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Adding a Tip

Gratly supports two tip entry modes depending on when the tip is collected:

- **Pre-tip (card payments):** The card reader prompts the customer to select a tip percentage or enter a custom tip amount *before* they authorize the payment. The tip is included in the authorization total. This is the default for card transactions.
- **Post-tip (cash or manual entry):** After payment is collected, a server or manager can enter the tip amount manually on the order summary screen. Use this for cash tips you want to record, or for situations where a card tip needs to be adjusted after the fact. Post-tip entry requires a manager PIN if the order has already been closed.

## Refunds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Transactions">
    Go to **Reports > Transactions** from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the transaction">
    Search by date, order number, or last four digits of the card to locate the transaction you need to refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Refund">
    Open the transaction detail and tap **Refund**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose full or partial refund">
    Select **Full Refund** to return the entire amount, or tap **Partial Refund** and enter the specific dollar amount to return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with manager PIN">
    Enter a manager or owner PIN to authorize the refund. The transaction record updates immediately to show the refund status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Card refunds are returned to the original payment card and may take **3–5 business days** to appear on the customer's statement, depending on their bank. Cash refunds should be issued from the drawer at the time of the refund.
</Note>

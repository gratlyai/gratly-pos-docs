Gratly POS gives you precise control over how you adjust guest checks — whether you're honoring a staff meal policy, recovering from a kitchen mistake, or correcting an order that was never fulfilled. Every discount, comp, and void is PIN-protected and automatically logged so your reporting stays accurate and your team stays accountable.

## Types of Discounts

Gratly supports three ways to reduce the amount a guest owes:

- **Percentage discount** — reduces the check total by a percentage of the subtotal (for example, a 10% staff discount or a 20% happy hour promotion).
- **Dollar discount** — reduces the check total by a fixed dollar amount (for example, $5 off a check for a promotional coupon).
- **Item comp** — removes the charge for a specific line item on the order and records it as a comp with a reason code for reporting purposes.

## Applying a Discount to a Check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    From the table layout or order list, tap the order you want to discount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Discount">
    Tap the **Discount** button on the order screen. A panel slides up showing your preset discounts and a custom entry option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or enter a discount">
    Tap a preset discount (such as "Staff Meal" or "Manager Comp"), or tap **Custom** and enter a specific percentage or dollar amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter manager PIN">
    Gratly prompts for a manager or owner PIN to authorize the adjustment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Apply">
    Tap **Apply**. The discount appears as a line item on the check and is recorded in the Discount & Comp report for that business day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creating Preset Discounts

Set up named discounts in advance so your staff can apply them quickly and consistently without entering custom amounts each time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Discount Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Discounts** from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new discount">
    Tap **+ New Discount** to open the discount creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and configure the discount">
    Enter a name (for example, "Staff Meal" or "10% Coupon"), then set the discount type (**Percentage** or **Dollar**) and the corresponding value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Toggle the discount **On** to make it available on the order screen, then tap **Save**. You can toggle any preset on or off at any time without deleting it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Comping an Item

Comp a specific item when you need to remove its charge from a check — for example, when a dish arrived incorrectly or a guest received a complimentary dessert. Comps are tracked separately from discounts in reporting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Long-press the item">
    On the order screen, press and hold the menu item you want to comp. A context menu appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Comp Item">
    Tap **Comp Item** from the context menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a reason">
    Choose a reason code from the list (for example, **Customer complaint**, **Kitchen error**, or **Complimentary**). Reason codes are required and appear in the Discount & Comp report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter manager PIN">
    Enter a manager or owner PIN to authorize the comp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The item is removed from the check total. A comp line is added to the order summary showing the item name, value, and reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Voiding an Order

A **void** cancels an entire order before payment has been collected. Use a void when an order was entered by mistake, a table walked out, or a takeout order was cancelled. A void removes the order entirely — it is not the same as a comp, which removes a single item from a check that still gets paid.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    Navigate to the order you need to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Void Order">
    Tap **Void Order** at the bottom of the order screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a reason">
    Choose a void reason from the list (for example, **Order entered in error**, **Customer cancelled**, or **Test order**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter manager PIN">
    Enter a manager or owner PIN to confirm the void.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order is removed">
    The order is deleted from the active table or queue and logged in the Void report. The table is freed up immediately on the floor layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  All discounts, comps, and voids are permanently logged and cannot be deleted from the audit trail. Managers can review the full history at any time in **Reports > Discounts**. Encourage staff to select accurate reason codes — this data directly informs your waste and loss reporting.
</Warning>

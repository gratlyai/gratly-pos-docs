---

This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new Gratly account to taking your first live order. By the end, you'll have your restaurant profile configured, your menu populated, your floor plan mapped, and your staff ready to serve — all in under 30 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.gratly.com](https://app.gratly.com) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your restaurant name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Create Account**. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Your Restaurant Profile">
    After signing in, you'll be prompted to finish your profile. Enter your restaurant's address, cuisine type, and service hours. Optionally upload a logo — it appears on all printed receipts and customer-facing screens. Click **Save Profile** when you're done.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your Menu">
    Navigate to **Menu > Categories** and create at least one category (for example, "Mains"). Then open that category and tap **Add Item** to create your first menu item — give it a name, price, and any modifiers you need. Repeat for each category and item. See the [Menu guide](/menu/overview) for full details on modifiers, pricing rules, and item availability.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Floor Plan">
    Go to **Settings > Floor Plan** and tap **Add Table**. Give each table a label (e.g., "Table 1" or "Patio 3"), then drag tables on the canvas to match your physical layout. You can create separate sections — such as "Dining Room" and "Bar" — to keep things organized.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a Staff Member">
    Go to **Settings > Staff** and click **Add Staff Member**. Enter the team member's name, email, and assign their role — Owner, Manager, Server, or Cashier. They'll receive an invitation email to set their own password. Repeat for every team member who needs access.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take Your First Order">
    From the POS home screen, tap any table on your floor plan to open an order. Browse your menu categories, tap items to add them, and make any modifier selections when prompted. When you're ready, tap **Send to Kitchen**. The order immediately appears on the Kitchen Display System (KDS) for your kitchen staff to action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before your first service, make sure your receipt printer, kitchen ticket printer, and KDS screen are connected and tested. Follow the [Hardware Setup guide](/hardware-setup) to get every device configured correctly.
</Tip>

The Gratly POS sales reporting dashboard gives you a real-time and historical view of your restaurant's revenue performance. Whether you need a quick daily snapshot or a detailed breakdown by item, server, or payment type, the reports section puts everything you need in one place — directly from your POS terminal or the Gratly web dashboard.

## Accessing Reports

From the POS terminal, tap the **Reports** tab in the bottom navigation bar. Viewing reports requires a **Manager** or **Owner** role — Servers and Cashiers do not have access to this section. You can also access the full reports suite from the Gratly web dashboard at [app.gratly.com](https://app.gratly.com), which offers additional export and scheduling options.

## Sales Summary Report

The Sales Summary is the default view when you open the Reports tab. It gives you a high-level overview of your restaurant's financial activity for the selected period.

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Gross Sales** | Total revenue before any discounts or taxes are applied |
| **Net Sales** | Revenue after discounts have been subtracted |
| **Tax Collected** | Total tax charged across all orders |
| **Total Payments** | Revenue broken down by payment method (cash, card, etc.) |
| **Average Check Value** | Net sales divided by the number of checks closed |
| **Number of Covers** | Total guests served during the period |

## Filtering Reports

Narrow your report to any time period or service type using the date picker and filter controls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the date picker">
    Tap the date range displayed at the top of any report view to open the date picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a time range">
    Choose one of the preset options — **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, or **This Month** — or tap **Custom Range** to enter specific start and end dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the range">
    Tap **Apply** to reload the report with your selected date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by service type or server">
    Tap the **Filter** button to narrow results further. You can filter by service type (**Dine-In** or **Takeout**) and by individual server to isolate specific segments of your sales data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Category & Item Breakdown

The **Item Sales** report shows a line-by-line breakdown of every menu item sold during the selected period. For each item, you'll see:

- **Quantity Sold** — how many units were ordered
- **Revenue** — total sales generated by that item
- **% of Total** — that item's share of overall net sales

Use this report to identify your top sellers and flag slow-moving items. Items that appear consistently at the bottom of the list are good candidates for menu review or promotional pricing.

## Payment Method Report

The **Payment Method** report breaks down your total payments by tender type — Cash, Card, Contactless, gift card, or any custom payment type you've configured. This report is particularly useful for:

- **Cash reconciliation** — compare expected cash collected against your drawer count
- **Card processing fees** — understand the volume going through each card type
- **Spotting anomalies** — unusual spikes in a particular payment method can flag errors or fraud

## Exporting Reports

You can export any report as a CSV (for use in spreadsheets) or a PDF (for printing or archiving).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report you want to export">
    Navigate to the specific report — Sales Summary, Item Sales, Payment Methods, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Export">
    Tap the **Export** button in the top-right corner of the report view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your format">
    Select **CSV** or **PDF** depending on how you plan to use the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your report">
    The report downloads directly to your device, or is emailed to the account's registered email address if you're exporting from the web dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Managers can schedule automatic daily email reports so your sales summary arrives in your inbox every morning without any manual steps. Go to **Settings > Reports > Scheduled Reports** to configure the delivery time and recipients.
</Tip>

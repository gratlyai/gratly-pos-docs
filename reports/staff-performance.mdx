Understanding how each team member is performing helps you coach more effectively, reward top performers, and catch issues before they become costly. The Staff Performance report in Gratly POS pulls together sales totals, order counts, discount usage, and tip data for every server and cashier — all in one view, filterable by any date range you choose.

## Accessing Staff Reports

From the POS terminal or the Gratly web dashboard, go to **Reports > Staff Performance**. Viewing this report requires a **Manager** or **Owner** role. The report defaults to the current day, but you can adjust the date range to view performance over any period.

## What the Report Shows

The Staff Performance overview lists every active staff member alongside their key metrics for the selected date range. Tap any staff member's name to drill into their individual detail report.

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Sales** | Combined revenue from all orders the staff member handled during the period |
| **Number of Orders** | Total count of checks opened and closed |
| **Average Check Value** | Total sales divided by number of orders — a useful measure of upselling effectiveness |
| **Covers Served** | Number of guests served (available when the staff member is assigned to dine-in tables) |
| **Discounts / Comps Applied** | Total dollar value and number of instances where a discount or comp was applied to an order |
| **Tips Collected** | Total tips recorded on that staff member's orders (only visible when tip tracking is enabled in Settings) |

## Filtering by Date and Staff

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Tap the date range at the top of the Staff Performance screen and select a preset — **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month** — or enter a **Custom Range** and tap **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a staff member">
    Tap any staff member's name in the list to open their individual detail report, which breaks down their performance order-by-order for the selected period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Discount & Comp Accountability

The **Discounts / Comps** column is one of the most actionable columns in the staff report. Use it to spot unusual patterns — for example, a staff member who is comping items or applying discounts far more frequently than their peers, or doing so without a manager PIN being entered.

If something looks out of the ordinary, cross-reference it with the full discount log in **Reports > Discounts**, which shows the exact order, item, discount type, and timestamp for every discount applied during the period.

## Clock-In / Clock-Out Records

If the **Timeclock** feature is enabled in your Gratly settings, each staff member's clock-in and clock-out times appear alongside their sales data in the detail view. This makes it straightforward to calculate labor cost per shift and compare it against that staff member's sales contribution for the day.

To enable timeclock tracking, go to **Settings > Staff > Timeclock**.

<Note>
  Staff report data is only as accurate as your role and PIN assignments. If multiple staff members share a login or forget to switch accounts between orders, their sales will be attributed to the wrong person. Make sure every team member logs in with their own unique PIN before starting their shift.
</Note>

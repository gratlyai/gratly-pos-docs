The end-of-day (EOD) process is how you officially close out a shift in Gratly POS. Running the EOD report lets you reconcile your cash drawer, review the day's sales totals, confirm your payment breakdown, and archive the shift so your records stay accurate and audit-ready. The process takes only a few minutes and should be completed by a Manager or Owner after the last order of the day is settled.

## When to Run the EOD Report

Run the EOD report after every last order of the day has been closed and paid — no open or pending orders should remain on the floor. You can run the EOD report from the **POS terminal** directly, or from the **Gratly web dashboard** at [app.gratly.com](https://app.gratly.com) if you prefer to close out remotely. Either method produces the same result and the same locked shift record.

## Running the EOD Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm all orders are closed">
    From the **Tables** or **Orders** screen, verify that no orders are still open or awaiting payment. Any unclosed orders will prevent the day from being finalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the End of Day screen">
    From the POS, tap **Reports** in the bottom navigation, then tap **End of Day**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the sales summary">
    Gratly displays a summary of the day's activity: gross sales, net sales, tax collected, and a full payment method breakdown. Review these figures before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Cash Drawer Count">
    Count all bills and coins in the drawer and enter the total when prompted. Gratly automatically calculates the **expected cash amount** based on recorded cash transactions and your opening drawer balance, then displays any **variance** between the two.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the Day">
    Once you've reviewed everything and accounted for any cash variance, tap **Close Day** to finalize the shift. The day is now locked and its data is archived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print or email the EOD report">
    After closing, Gratly prompts you to **Print** the EOD report to your receipt printer or **Email** a copy to the account's registered email address. You can do both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cash Variance

If the cash amount you counted doesn't match what Gratly expected, the difference is displayed in **red** on the Cash Drawer Count screen. A variance doesn't block you from closing the day, but you should record the reason before confirming.

Common reasons for a cash variance include:

- **Safe drop** — cash moved to a safe mid-shift
- **Staff payout** — petty cash paid out during the shift
- **Counting error** — recount the drawer before logging a discrepancy

All variances are automatically logged with a timestamp and the name of the staff member who ran the EOD. You can review the full variance history in **Reports > Cash Management**.

## What the EOD Report Includes

The finalized EOD report contains a complete picture of the day's operations:

- **Sales by category, item, and payment method** — the same breakdowns available in the standard sales reports
- **Cash drawer opening balance, closing count, and variance**
- **Total discounts and comps applied** — with a count of instances
- **Staff summary** — order count per server for the shift
- **Void log** — a record of every order or item that was voided during the day

<Warning>
  Closing the day is permanent. Once you tap **Close Day**, that shift's data is archived and can no longer be edited — orders cannot be modified, voids cannot be reversed, and the cash count cannot be updated. Always take a moment to review the full summary before you confirm.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  To avoid the manual step each night, you can schedule an automatic EOD summary email to be delivered to your inbox after close. Set this up in **Settings > Reports > Scheduled Reports** and choose your preferred delivery time.
</Tip>
